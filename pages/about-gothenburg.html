About Gothenburg

Sweden's friendly city on the west coast

Gothenburg is relaxed, green and easy to get around on foot or by tram. Think cosy cafés, great seafood, a lively food and craft beer scene, and the sea always close by. Early June brings long, light evenings, so stay a few extra days and enjoy it.

DON'T MISS

The archipelago

Just 30 minutes from the city centre you are out among islands, fishing villages and smooth granite rocks by the sea. In early June it is perfect for a walk, a swim if you dare, and seafood on a jetty.

🏝️

Southern islands

Car-free islands such as Styrsö, Vrångö and Brännö. Take tram 11 to Saltholmen and hop on the ferry. Your public transport ticket works on the boat. Southern islands guide →

⚓

Northern islands

Hönö, Öckerö and further out with harbours, bathing spots and fish restaurants. Reach them by bus and ferry. Northern islands guide →

🦐

Boat tours and seafood cruises

Let someone else drive: guided archipelago tours, shrimp cruises and seafood safaris along the coast. Boat tours → · Seafood cruises →

In the city

☕

Haga and fika

Walk the old wooden-house district of Haga and try the famous giant cinnamon buns. Fika, the Swedish coffee break, is a must.

🐟

Seafood

Fresh shrimp, oysters and fish from the west coast. Visit the "fish church" Feskekôrka market hall, or pick one of the fish restaurants.

🛶

Canal tour

See the city from the water on a flat Paddan boat through the old canals and out into the harbour. Paddan tours →

🎢

Liseberg

Scandinavia's big amusement park, right in the city centre. Fun in the evenings, also without the rides.

🌳

Slottsskogen

The city's big green park, with a free animal park, picnic lawns and running paths. A lovely break after a day of lectures.

🚗

World of Volvo

Discover Volvo's story and classic cars at the World of Volvo experience centre.
