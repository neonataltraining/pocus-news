YOUR FOUR DAYS

Two venues

Lectures and workshops at the harbour, then a full hands-on day at the children's hospital.

Days 1–3 · Mon–Wed

Conference Centre Campus Nya Varvet

At the harbour in west Gothenburg. Bus 114 runs almost to the door.

Day 4 · Thursday

Queen Silvia Children's Hospital

Clinical Training Centre (KTC), Östra sjukhuset. A course bus takes you there from the Conference Centre Campus Nya Varvet. Look for the colourful, greenish children's hospital and follow the signs to the Clinical Training Centre.
