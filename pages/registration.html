About VAT: All prices are excluding Swedish VAT (25%).
• Workplace outside the EU: no VAT is added.
• Workplace in another EU country: no VAT is added if you give us your hospital's or institution's VAT number when you register. Without it, Swedish VAT is added.
• Workplace in Sweden: Swedish VAT (moms) is added, but Swedish hospitals can deduct it, so in the end your hospital does not pay it.
Good to know before you register
• Plans change, we understand. If you cancel in writing before 1 March 2027, you get your money back minus 500 SEK. After that we can't refund, but a colleague from your institution can take your place at any time (500 SEK). Visa or travel problems do not give a right to a refund.
• You pay by card (VISA/MasterCard) when you register.
• The reduced price depends on where you work, not your nationality. We may ask for a short confirmation from your employer.
• Part of the course is with real patients, so please keep all patient information confidential and don't take photos or recordings of patients.
• If you feel unwell with signs of an infection, please tell us. You may not be able to join the patient sessions, but we'll find another way for you to train.
• We film the lectures and take photos for our website. Just tell us if you'd rather not be in them.
• Travel, accommodation and insurance are arranged by you.
