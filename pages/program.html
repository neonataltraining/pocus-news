National and local lecturers and instructors

Our local lecturers and instructors are the Neonatal POCUS team from Gothenburg, with colleagues from Paediatric Intensive Care and Paediatric Cardiology. Together with our international faculty and trainers from across Sweden, they lead the workshops and the hands-on day, so you learn in small groups from very experienced clinicians who use POCUS in the NICU every day.
