Gothenburg is Sweden's friendly second city on the west coast, easy to explore on foot or by tram. Stroll along the canals and the Haga district with its cafés and famous cinnamon buns, enjoy fresh seafood, or visit the Liseberg amusement park. Early June is a lovely time of year, with long light evenings. Organizer

The course is run by the Neonatal Unit, Queen Silvia Children's Hospital, Sahlgrenska University Hospital, Gothenburg. Commee AB is the conference organizer for the Neonatal Unit and handles registration and payment.

Hands-on training

Scan, measure and discuss in small groups of 5–6 participants. Simulator stations, real cases, echo measurements and supervised scanning of patients (when available), covering TnEcho/NPE, lung ultrasound, GI ultrasound and vascular access.

Programme and faculty

Learn from international faculty and world-leading experts in neonatal haemodynamics and POCUS, together with experienced local instructors and lecturers from Gothenburg and across Sweden.
The programme is preliminary. Speakers or times may change, which does not give a right to a refund.

Online participation

Lectures are streamed live in Microsoft Teams. Workshops are not streamed. All participants can watch the lecture recordings for 4 weeks after the course.
The course is organised by a team of doctors, nurses and administrators from the Neonatal Unit, who also run the technology. Small interruptions or technical problems may occur.

Reduced fees

For participants working in low- or lower-middle-income countries (World Bank). It depends on your workplace, not your nationality. We may ask for proof.

Cancellation

Registration is binding. Cancel in writing before 1 March 2027 and you get a refund minus 500 SEK. After that, no refunds, but a colleague from your institution can take your place at any time (500 SEK). Visa or travel problems do not give a right to a refund.
