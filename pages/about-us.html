About us

Neonatal Unit · Queen Silvia Children's Hospital · Sahlgrenska University Hospital

Caring for the smallest patients in West Sweden

Our Neonatal Unit at Sahlgrenska University Hospital in Gothenburg is one of the largest in Sweden. We care for newborns who need advanced treatment right after birth, and we are the main referral centre for a large part of West Sweden. As a level IV unit, we are also a referral centre for paediatric surgery and paediatric cardiac surgery.

Teaching and research

Together with the University of Gothenburg we train students and professionals, and we are active in neonatal research and new ideas in care.
