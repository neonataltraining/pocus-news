Four days by the sea

Gothenburg is Sweden's harbour city on the west coast. The course is held at Campus Nya Varvet, a historic naval base right on the waterfront, and the course hotel is by the water too. Early June is a lovely time to come: long, light evenings, and the car-free islands of the archipelago are only a short ferry ride away.
